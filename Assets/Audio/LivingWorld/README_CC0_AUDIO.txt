THE VOXEL BOX - Living World Audio

Version 0.4 works without external audio files: footsteps, wind, rain and bird calls are generated procedurally at runtime.

Recommended CC0 replacements researched for a later recording-quality pack:
- Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone), OpenGameArt, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
- Forest bird sounds by pauliuw, OpenGameArt, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/forest-bird-sounds
- Park ambiences by Thimras, OpenGameArt, CC0 (birds / river / wind)
  https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences

No third-party audio binary is redistributed in this build.
